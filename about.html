OUR STORY

About Maza Gaon

A digital initiative designed to connect villagers with information and community services.

THE PROBLEM

Many problems. One community.

Villages often face challenges related to agriculture, healthcare, education, hygiene, water supply, electricity and access to government schemes.

Information is often scattered across different places, making it difficult for residents to find what they need.

💡

Our Objective

To create one simple digital platform where villagers can find useful information, access resources and report community problems.

OUR GOALS

What Maza Gaon Wants to Achieve

🌾 Support Farmers

Make agricultural information easier to access.

🏥 Improve Awareness

Promote health and hygiene awareness.

📚 Encourage Education

Connect students with useful resources.

🤝 Build Community

Give people a way to communicate local problems.
